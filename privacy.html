CyberRakshak – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

CyberRakshak is provided as a citizen-facing cyber-safety companion on behalf of Chandigarh Police. This app does not collect or store personal data beyond what is strictly needed to process a chat message or a scan request you initiate.

Chat messages

Chat messages you send are transmitted to the CyberRakshak backend to generate a response. The active conversation is held in app memory only and is not saved to device storage. Backend processing and retention are governed by its own policy.

Scanner (files and links)

Files or links you submit to the Scanner are transmitted only for the purpose of that safety check and are not used for any other purpose by this app.

Quizzes

Quiz answers are sent to check correctness. If you opt to save your best quiz scores, they are stored only on this device and are not transmitted.

Location

Location access, if granted, is used only to center the map on the Locate Help Nearby screen and is never stored or transmitted.

AI-assisted tools and accuracy

CyberRakshak uses AI-assisted tools to provide cyber-safety awareness and preliminary checks. The information and results provided may not always be fully accurate and are not guaranteed to be 100% accurate. This app is intended for awareness and guidance purposes only and should not be treated as a definitive security assessment or a substitute for official verification.

Guidance and reporting

This app is a guidance tool and does not replace filing a formal complaint. For financial cybercrime, report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

CyberRakshak, provided on behalf of Chandigarh Police.
